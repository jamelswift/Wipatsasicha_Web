Bone Trail mini game built with HTML5 Canvas. Move your mouse (or finger on mobile) to guide the bone trail, collect stars, and avoid red hazards before time runs out.

How to play

  • Move mouse on desktop or drag on mobile to steer the bone trail.
  • Collect pickups to gain score and more time.
  • Press Space to activate boost when Boost is Ready.
  • Hit Restart to begin a new run any time.

Rules

  • Yellow Star: score up with combo bonus.
  • Blue Gem: extra score and extra time.
  • Green Clock: large time bonus.
  • Red hazards reduce time and reset combo.
  • Game ends when time reaches 0.
